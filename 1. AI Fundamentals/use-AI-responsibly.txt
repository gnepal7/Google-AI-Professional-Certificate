# Responsible AI best practices
  + Take steps to protect sensitive information
    . Use generic placeholders
    . Frame prompts
    . Input only relevant context
    . Clear AI's memory
  + Understand regulatory and compliance boundaries
    . Does this task have regulatory requirements?
    . Are there licensing or credential requirements?
    . Who is responsible?
  + Build accountability into your process
    . Track your sources
    . Make your process auditable
    . Document your verification steps
  + Use the ACT checklist
    > A stands for “Ask yourself” 
      . Is AI right for this task?
      . Is this data safe to use?
      . Am I following the rules?
    > C stands for “Check before you use the output” 
      . For accuracy
      . For bias and objectivity
      . For appropriateness
      . For originality and judgment
    > T stands for “Tell people when you use AI” 
      . Be transparent
      . Be complaint
