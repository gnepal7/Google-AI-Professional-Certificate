# Fundamentals of machine learning
  + Understand artificial intelligence

  + Understand machine learning
    - A technique that enables a tool to find patterns and learn from data on its own.
  + Approaches to machine learning
    1. Supervised Learning : large dataset that has been labeled by people
    2. Unsupervised Learning : dataset has not been labeled by people
    3. Reinforcement Learning : train AI through a trial-and-error process
      that is guided by a reward system. 

# Understand AI limitations
  + Understand biases in AI
    > To avoid unfair bias in outputs:
      . Be specific about the output you want
      . Use follow-up prompts to correct outputs that seem biased or inaccurate
  + The constraint of knowledge cutoff
    > To work effectively with a model's knowledge cutoff, you can use these techniques:
      . Look up the cutoff
      . Verify time-sensitive information
      . Specify your timeframe
      . Refine with follow-up prompts
  + Changes in AI’s performance over time
    > Drift is the gradual decline in a model’s accuracy and relevance as the real world changes. 
      You might observe drift in two ways:
      . Factual drift
      . Behavioral drift
    > Here are a few ways to manage and mitigate both kinds of drift:
      . Provide accurate and up-to-date context in your prompts
      . Keep chats focused by starting a new conversation for each specific task.
      . Be explicit
