# Prompting tips and tricks
  + Follow the three C’s when writing your prompts:
    . Be concise
    . Be clear
    . Be consistent
  + Always evaluate the output, focus on such as:
    . Accuracy
    . Bias
    . Relevancy
    . Consistency
  + Improve your results through iteration
    . Revisit the prompting framework
    . Break up complex tasks
    . Add constraints
